# Homogenization

## Summary

Homogenization is the concentration of many downstream systems on the same small set of model architectures, data sources, and pre-trained checkpoints.

## Explanation

[[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] argues that homogenization creates leverage: one model improvement can help many tasks. It also creates fragility: the same bias, security issue, or failure mode can propagate into many applications.
