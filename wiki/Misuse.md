# Misuse

## Summary

Misuse is the intentional or negligent use of language models for harmful purposes, such as misinformation, abuse, fraud, manipulation, or unsafe decision support.

## Explanation

[[Language Models are Few-Shot Learners]] discusses malicious applications of generated text. [[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] expands misuse into a broad sociotechnical risk. [[Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback]] notes a sharp tension: better instruction following can make systems more useful and also easier to misuse.
