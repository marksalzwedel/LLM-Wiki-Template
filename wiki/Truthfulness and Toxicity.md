# Truthfulness and Toxicity

## Summary

Truthfulness and toxicity are safety-relevant output qualities: whether a model fabricates or misleads, and whether it generates harmful or abusive content.

## Explanation

[[Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback]] reports improvements in truthfulness and reductions in toxic output generation for InstructGPT models. [[Language Models are Few-Shot Learners]] and [[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] both identify harmful generation, bias, and misuse as important risks of large language models.
