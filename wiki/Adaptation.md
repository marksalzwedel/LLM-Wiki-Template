# Adaptation

## Summary

Adaptation is the process of turning a broad pre-trained model into a useful system for a particular task, domain, or user setting.

## Explanation

Adaptation includes [[Fine-Tuning]], [[Prompting]], task heads, retrieval systems, filters, human oversight, and application-specific rules. [[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] stresses that deployment often needs more than a base model.

## Contradictions and Tensions

- [[BERT]] adapts by updating weights for tasks, [[GPT-3]] adapts behavior through context, and [[InstructGPT]] adapts model behavior to preference data. These are competing but compatible adaptation strategies.
