# Emergence

## Summary

Emergence refers to capabilities or behaviors that appear at larger scale and are difficult to predict from smaller models.

## Explanation

[[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] presents emergence as both an opportunity and a risk. [[GPT-3]] gives a concrete example: scaling improves many forms of [[Few-Shot Learning]], including tasks not explicitly trained as supervised objectives.

## Contradictions and Tensions

- [[Language Models are Few-Shot Learners]] mostly treats emergent-like behavior as a capability result. [[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] stresses that unpredictable capabilities make evaluation and governance harder.
