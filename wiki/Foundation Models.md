# Foundation Models

## Summary

Foundation models are broad pre-trained models that can be adapted to many downstream tasks.

## Explanation

[[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] uses this term for models such as [[BERT]] and [[GPT-3]]. The name emphasizes that these models are central infrastructure but incomplete: they require [[Adaptation]], [[Evaluation]], safety work, and domain-specific judgment before deployment.

## Contradictions and Tensions

- The foundation-model framing does not contradict BERT or GPT-3 technically; it widens the unit of analysis from model performance to ecosystems of reuse, inherited defects, power concentration, and social impact.
