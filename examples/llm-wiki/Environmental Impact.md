# Environmental Impact

## Summary

Environmental impact refers to the energy use, compute cost, emissions, and material footprint of training and deploying large models.

## Explanation

[[Language Models are Few-Shot Learners]] includes energy usage among broader impacts. [[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] treats environmental cost as a major society-level concern of scaling and widespread deployment.
