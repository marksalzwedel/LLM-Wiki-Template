# Scaling Language Models

## Summary

Scaling language models increases parameters, data, and compute to improve capability, generality, or sample efficiency.

## Explanation

[[BERT]] reports gains from larger pre-trained models on downstream tasks. [[Language Models are Few-Shot Learners]] makes scale central by showing broad few-shot gains from [[GPT-3]]. [[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] argues that scale also produces [[Emergence]] and [[Homogenization]].

## Contradictions and Tensions

- [[Language Models are Few-Shot Learners]] emphasizes capability gains from scale. [[Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback]] argues that scale alone does not ensure helpfulness, honesty, harmlessness, or instruction following.
- The foundation-model report adds that scale can increase environmental cost and concentrate shared risks.
