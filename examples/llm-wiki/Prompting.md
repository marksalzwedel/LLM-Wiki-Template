# Prompting

## Summary

Prompting is the practice of specifying a task, context, examples, or constraints in text given to a language model.

## Explanation

[[GPT-3]] turns prompts into the primary interface for task performance. [[InstructGPT]] later improves the model's ability to respond to instruction-like prompts by adding [[Supervised Fine-Tuning]] and [[Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback]].
