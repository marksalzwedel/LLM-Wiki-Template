# Bias and Fairness

## Summary

Bias and fairness concern how model outputs represent, disadvantage, stereotype, or distribute harm across people and groups.

## Explanation

[[Language Models are Few-Shot Learners]] discusses fairness, bias, and representation in generated language. [[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] argues that homogenized foundation models can propagate the same biases into many downstream systems. [[Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback]] includes biased and toxic outputs among behaviors alignment work should reduce.

## Contradictions and Tensions

- Scaling and broad pre-training can improve capability while also absorbing social biases from data. Alignment and filtering can mitigate some harms but may introduce new normative choices about whose preferences count.
