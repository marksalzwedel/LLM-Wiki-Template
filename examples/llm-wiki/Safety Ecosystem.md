# Safety Ecosystem

## Summary

The safety ecosystem is the collection of technical, organizational, policy, and deployment practices needed to manage model risks.

## Explanation

[[Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback]] frames RLHF as one useful tool, not a complete safety solution. [[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] broadens this into a landscape of evaluation, alignment, robustness, security, privacy, fairness, legality, misuse prevention, and domain governance.

## Contradictions and Tensions
