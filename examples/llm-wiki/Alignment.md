# Alignment

## Summary

Alignment is the effort to make model behavior match user intent, human preferences, and safety constraints.

## Explanation

[[Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback]] defines a practical alignment path for language models: demonstrations, comparisons, [[Reward Models]], and [[Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback]]. [[On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models]] treats alignment as part of a larger safety and governance problem.

## Contradictions and Tensions

- [[GPT-3]] demonstrates broad competence from pre-training and prompting, but [[InstructGPT]] shows that competence does not imply alignment.
- Alignment work can improve helpfulness while also making harmful instructions easier to execute if safeguards fail.
